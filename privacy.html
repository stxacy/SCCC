Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Who we are

Surf Coast Cricket Club (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a community cricket club based at Banyul-Warri Fields Sporting Precinct, Merrijig Drive, North Torquay VIC 3228. You can contact us at info@surfcoastcricketclub.com.

What information we collect

We only collect personal information you choose to provide to us, specifically:

  • Contact form submissions — your first name, last name, email address, and message. Subject line is optional.

We do not run advertising, build user profiles, or collect information through tracking pixels or analytics tools on this website.

How we use your information

Information submitted through our contact form is used solely to respond to your enquiry. We will not share your details with third parties for marketing purposes, nor will we add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent.

Third-party services

This website uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Google Fonts — fonts are loaded from Google’s servers. Google may log the request. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • FormSubmit.co — our contact form submissions are processed by FormSubmit. See FormSubmit’s Privacy Policy.
  • Behold.so — our Instagram feed is served via Behold. Posts are fetched from Instagram / Meta. See Behold’s Privacy Policy.
  • Microlink.io — used to fetch news article thumbnails from external URLs. See Microlink’s Privacy Policy.
  • Instagram / YouTube / Facebook embeds & links — clicking through to these platforms is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Cookies and local storage

This website uses browser localStorage to remember whether you have dismissed our cookie notice. This does not collect any personal data and is not shared with any third party.

Third-party services listed above may set their own cookies when you interact with embedded content or click external links. We do not control these cookies.

Data retention

Contact form enquiries are received directly into our club email inbox and retained only as long as necessary to respond. We do not maintain a separate database of enquiries.

Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act 1988, you have the right to request access to, or correction of, any personal information we hold about you. To make a request, email us at info@surfcoastcricketclub.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
