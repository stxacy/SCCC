Our Story

More than a club —
a community

Surf Coast Cricket Club is a newly established, community-minded and inclusive club in the growing area of North Torquay. Based at the Banyul-Warri Fields Sporting Precinct on Merrijig Drive, we compete in the GCA.

Formed through the merger of the Surf Coast Stingrays and the Surf Coast Suns Football Netball Club, we field 4 senior men’s sides and a growing junior program. We acknowledge the Wadawurrung People as the Traditional Custodians of the land on which we play.

We are a club built on inclusion. Whether you’re a seasoned cricketer or picking up a bat for the first time, there is a place for you at Surf Coast Cricket Club.
