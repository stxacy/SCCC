Who We Are

A new club with deep roots

Surf Coast Cricket Club is a newly established, community-minded and inclusive club in the growing area of North Torquay. Based at the Banyul-Warri Fields Sporting Precinct on Merrijig Drive, we compete in the Geelong Cricket Association.

Formed through the merger of the Surf Coast Stingrays and the Surf Coast Suns Football Netball Club, we field four senior men’s sides and a growing junior program across U/11, U/13, U/15 and U/17. We’re a club that welcomes everyone — whether you’ve played your whole life or are picking up a bat for the first time.

Acknowledgement of Country

Surf Coast Cricket Club acknowledges the Wadawurrung People as the Traditional Custodians of the land on which we play and train. We pay our respects to their Elders past, present and emerging, and recognise their ongoing connection to land, waters and culture.
